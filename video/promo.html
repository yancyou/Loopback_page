Looped Transformers apply the same blocks again and again
One interface, three ways to reuse the updates

Residual addition

Every stored update keeps weight 1.

Joint softmax (AttnRes)

Input and updates share one unit of weight.

Loopback

Input stays at 1; decay sets the total, attention allocates it.

The recipe
Loopback in three moves
1Store each block's update δ, not its full output.
2Keep the shared stack's input on its own path, at weight 1.
3Decay sets the total update weight; attention allocates it.
\( \displaystyle \boldsymbol h_{s+1}=\boldsymbol h_0+\sum_{i\le s} c_{s,i}\,\boldsymbol\delta_i,\qquad \sum_{i\le s} c_{s,i}=B_s \)
At every visit end Bs ≤ Kλ/(1−λ). Zero queries give the decay-only loop; λ = 1 with zero queries gives ordinary addition.
